C# meteo sim on WindowsForms of .NET FrameWork

Given a time interval (start date) check:

-how many times the temperature has increased between one measurement and the previous one;
-if the temperature has dropped below zero at least once;
-how many times the temperature has exceeded the average of the previous day;
-the average humidity;
-the prevailing wind direction
-the forecast for the next 12 hours based on the trend of atmospheric pressures.

The SW simulates the temperature measurement of 30 or more days using a random number generator.
